# Cross-Functional Team Structure

## Introduction

This document outlines the cross-functional team structure for the CPI Hub project. The organization is designed to promote collaboration, efficiency, and innovation while delivering high-quality software that meets stakeholder needs. By bringing together diverse skill sets and perspectives, we aim to create a cohesive team environment that can tackle complex challenges across the entire project lifecycle.

## Organizational Principles

The CPI Hub team structure is built on the following principles:

1. **Product-Centric Approach**: Teams are organized around product capabilities rather than technical functions.
2. **End-to-End Ownership**: Teams are responsible for their features from conception to production.
3. **Cross-Functional Composition**: Each team includes diverse skills needed to deliver complete solutions.
4. **Autonomous Decision-Making**: Teams have authority to make decisions within their domain.
5. **Collaborative Problem-Solving**: Open communication across teams to share knowledge and solve complex problems.
6. **Continuous Improvement**: Regular retrospectives and adaptation of team structure and processes.
7. **Customer Centricity**: Direct engagement with users to understand needs and get feedback.

## Team Structure Overview

The CPI Hub project is organized into the following team types:

### Core Product Teams

Product-focused teams that own specific functional domains of the CPI Hub platform.

### Platform Teams

Infrastructure and platform teams that provide shared services and tooling.

### Support Teams

Specialized teams that provide expertise and support across product teams.

### Leadership Team

Strategic direction and organizational coordination.

## Cross-Team Collaboration

### Communities of Practice

To foster knowledge sharing and consistency across teams, the following Communities of Practice (CoPs) will be established:

1. **Engineering CoP**: Sharing technical best practices, architecture patterns, and coding standards
2. **UX/UI CoP**: Aligning design systems, user experience patterns, and research methodologies
3. **Data Science CoP**: Sharing ML models, algorithms, and data processing techniques
4. **Quality CoP**: Aligning on testing approaches, tools, and quality metrics
5. **DevOps CoP**: Sharing infrastructure, deployment, and operational best practices

### Coordination Mechanisms

The following mechanisms will facilitate cross-team coordination:

1. **Scrum of Scrums**: Regular meeting of team representatives to coordinate dependencies
2. **Architecture Review Board**: Oversight of technical decisions with cross-cutting impacts
3. **Product Council**: Alignment of product roadmaps and priorities
4. **Design System Committee**: Maintenance of consistent design patterns and components
5. **Technical Steering Committee**: Strategic technical direction and standards

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Product Owner

- Defines product vision and roadmap
- Prioritizes backlog and represents user needs
- Collaborates with stakeholders to gather requirements
- Accepts completed work and provides feedback
- Measures and communicates product success

### Technical Lead

- Guides technical implementation and architecture
- Makes technical decisions within team domain
- Mentors team members on technical practices
- Ensures code quality and technical standards
- Resolves technical impediments

### Engineers (Frontend, Backend, Full-Stack)

- Design and implement features and services
- Write clean, maintainable, and tested code
- Conduct code reviews and pair programming
- Solve technical problems and debug issues
- Participate in planning and estimation activities

### QA Engineers

- Design and execute test plans
- Develop automated tests
- Report and track defects
- Verify bug fixes and feature implementations
- Provide quality metrics and insights

### UX Designers

- Create user flows and wireframes
- Design visual and interaction patterns
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create prototypes and design specifications
- Ensure consistent user experience across the platform

### Data Scientists / ML Engineers

- Develop data models and algorithms
- Train and evaluate machine learning models
- Implement data processing pipelines
- Analyze data patterns and insights
- Optimize model performance and accuracy

### DevOps Engineers

- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Configure and manage cloud infrastructure
- Monitor system performance and availability
- Implement automation for deployment and operations
- Troubleshoot production issues

### Technical Writers

- Create user-facing documentation
- Develop API and developer documentation
- Maintain knowledge base and internal documentation
- Create diagrams and visual aids
- Ensure documentation accuracy and completeness

## Agile Practices

The CPI Hub teams will follow these agile practices:

1. **Scrum Framework**: 2-week sprints with planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
2. **Kanban Boards**: Visual workflow management for tracking progress and bottlenecks
3. **User Stories**: Requirements expressed from user perspective with acceptance criteria
4. **Continuous Integration**: Automated testing and code quality checks on each commit
5. **Continuous Delivery**: Automated deployment pipelines for frequent, reliable releases
6. **Pair Programming**: Collaborative coding for knowledge sharing and quality
7. **Test-Driven Development**: Writing tests before implementation to ensure quality
8. **Mob Programming**: Whole-team collaboration on complex problems

## Team Evolution

The team structure is designed to evolve as the project progresses:

### Initial Phase (0-6 months)

- Focus on core platform capabilities
- Smaller teams with more shared responsibilities
- Higher leadership involvement in team activities
- Emphasis on establishing working practices and tools

### Growth Phase (6-12 months)

- Expansion of specialized roles
- Formation of dedicated product teams
- Refinement of cross-team coordination
- Development of communities of practice

### Maturity Phase (12+ months)

- Optimization of team boundaries based on learned dependencies
- Increased team autonomy and self-organization
- Evolution of support functions based on identified needs
- Streamlined governance and decision-making processes

## Conclusion

This cross-functional team structure provides a framework for organizing the CPI Hub project to maximize collaboration, innovation, and delivery. The structure is designed to be adaptable, enabling teams to evolve their working practices based on project needs and organizational learning. By bringing together diverse skills within product-focused teams, supported by specialized platform and support teams, we aim to create an environment where high-quality software can be delivered efficiently and effectively.
